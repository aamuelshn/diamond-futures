05 THE CURVE ENGINE

Transparent enough to challenge.

Diamond Futures does not reprint a published projection. It estimates present talent, applies age-specific changes learned from historical careers, models playing time and MLB survival separately, and preserves correlated outcomes across each simulated path.

  1. 01Talent nowThree-season recency weighting, sample-size regression, and Statcast expected performance.
  2. 02Aging nextEmpirical transitions trained on modern SABR/Lahman player-seasons.
  3. 03Role & survivalPlaying time, role security, and exit from MLB are modeled separately from skill.
  4. 04Uncertain futures5,000 correlated simulations produce ranges and milestone odds.
